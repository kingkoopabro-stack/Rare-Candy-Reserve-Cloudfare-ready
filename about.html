About the Reserve

One shop. Every corner of the hobby.

Rare Candy Reserve is a one-person shop out of Starke, Florida — graded and hard-to-find Pokémon and other trading cards, mint comics, custom 3D printing, tech builds, cosplay gear, and antiques. Thousands of cards in stock, from out-of-print sets to fresh slabs. Every graded card is professionally authenticated before it ships, and every print comes off the same tuned production setup in the shop.

The shop is a Vita Nova Records property. The idea is simple: collect from the best of the best — fair prices, honest descriptions, and a little something extra in every order.

About the owner

The first apartment.

Chris has always tried to bring out the creativity in people and build with others, hoping to make something great. He leaned more on tech — coding and organizing — with a hefty dose of ADHD and ADD, so he didn't always have to rely on people he slowly grew apart from. He still talks to most of these people to this day.

He wants to build a place for everyone, like he always has — and that's exactly what he's doing, spending his time working on it, so people once more have a place to feel safe doing what they love, profiting off it, and making sure everyone owns a piece of the pie.

About the Reserve

One shop. Every corner of the hobby.

Rare Candy Reserve is a one-person shop out of Starke, Florida — graded and hard-to-find Pokémon and other trading cards, mint comics, custom 3D printing, tech builds, cosplay gear, and antiques. Thousands of cards in stock, from out-of-print sets to fresh slabs. Every graded card is professionally authenticated before it ships, and every print comes off the same tuned production setup in the shop.

The shop is a Vita Nova Records property. The idea is simple: collect from the best of the best — fair prices, honest descriptions, and a little something extra in every order.

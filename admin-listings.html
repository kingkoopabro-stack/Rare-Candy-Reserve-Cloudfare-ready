Blank means the Stripe link charges the item price only (buyer arranges shipping with you separately). Set 0 for free shipping. The charge is added as its own line on the Stripe link, and Florida tax is collected on it too.

First photo is the one buyers see on the card. JPG, PNG, WebP, or GIF — resized automatically to fit. If your photo is HEIC, screenshot it first.

Show it in action — a spin, a demo, a closer look. MP4, WebM, or MOV, under 15 MB. Buyers watch it on the listing's detail page.

Each dropdown is one choice buyers make. The price change is added to the base price — 0 for no change, 10 for +$10, -5 for $5 off. If a dropdown changes the price, buyers pay by PayPal (Stripe links are fixed-price, so the Stripe button hides itself when the total moves).

Leave it blank and saving makes one for you (once Stripe is connected). Paste a link by hand and it stays as-is — it will never be replaced automatically.

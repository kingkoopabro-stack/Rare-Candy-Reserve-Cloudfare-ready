🃏 Card Check

Is this card real — or does it look like a fake?

Post a picture of a card and the community weighs in. Every post carries a title, a description, and flair so people know what they’re looking at — and the shop gives it an AI first look (advisory only, never a guarantee) before the votes come in.

Ask the community

Post a card for checking

How it works

Real · Looks Fake · Not Sure

1 · You post a clear photo, a title, and a description.
2 · The shop runs an AI first look and posts it as an advisory note.
3 · Members vote Real, Looks Fake, or Not Sure — and comment with GIFs.
4 · The votes and the comments stay on the post so the next person can learn from it.

A clear, well-lit front-and-back photo gets the best answers. Blurry photos get asked for a reshoot.

🛒 Market — sell your cards & collectibles

List it free. Sell it fair.

Anyone can sell their own Pokémon cards and collectibles here once their account and payout method are set up. Listing is free — a small fair cut goes to Rare Candy Reserve only when the item sells. Resale is welcome. No firearms, nothing illegal, ever.

Seller setup

Account + payout, then sell

Set up your account and a payout method once, then list as much as you like — free. When an item sells, a small fair cut goes to Rare Candy Reserve and the rest goes to you through your payout method. Payments are arranged directly with the seller until the automated payout is live.

The card is the item. Bumper, stand, or figure extras are a free courtesy by message only — never the thing being sold.

🔄 Up for Trade

Offer a trade. Let the community weigh in.

A dedicated area to offer cards up for trade. Every trade listing needs a photo and a check first — the shop verifies no fakes or proxies before a trade goes live, using the best tools available. Then members vote whether it’s a good trade, a fair one, or a bad deal, and comment if the poster allows it.

Offer a trade

Put a card up for trade

Trade safety

Checked before it goes live

1 · Photo required — no photo, no trade listing.
2 · The shop checks the card for fakes and proxies before it’s listed.
3 · Members vote: Good trade · Fair · Bad deal.
4 · Comments open only if the poster allows them.

A trade that looks fake or proxy gets blocked, not listed. That’s the whole point.

🛡 Rules — keep everyone safe

Sell what you want. Keep it safe.

🚫

No hate speech

No hate speech in communities or on pages — ever. Pages are customizable to a degree, but the rules aren’t.

🔒

No outside sales

Don’t take deals off the platform to dodge the rules. Outside sales risk a permanent IP ban of the account.

⛔

Nothing illegal

No firearms, nothing illegal at all — not in the market, not in trades, not in the flea market, nowhere.

🃏

No fakes or proxies

Trades and listings get checked before they go live. Fakes and proxies don’t get listed.

After Dark — planned, not open. The adult “after dark market” is coming later: sellers prove ID and age through a secure third-party route, content is hosted in an encrypted vault, and previews are watermark-blurred so they can’t be stolen and redistributed. It is not open yet, and nothing is listed there today.

Cakewalk Sonar

Cakewalk Sonar — official image from cakewalk.com.

Sonar is the new Cakewalk — the evolution of the free Cakewalk by BandLab. A full desktop DAW for Windows 11 and up: unlimited tracks, ProChannel console strips, VocalSync, and AudioSnap. BandLab vs Cakewalk, quick version: BandLab is the studio in your browser and your pocket — record, mix, and collab from anywhere, free with an account. Cakewalk Sonar is the full studio on your Windows PC — deeper mixing and editing for finished songs. Same family, different jobs.

Horticulture

Learn to grow plants — from houseplants to a full nursery — with real hands-on guidance. Growers sell plants, seeds, and cuttings online and at markets, all from their own yard.

Free tools + know-how

  • UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions — the University of Florida's free, research-based gardening guides for Florida: what to plant, when, and how. Start here before anywhere else.
  • USDA Plant Hardiness Zone Map — look up your zone by ZIP code. North Florida is mostly zones 8a–9a. Your zone tells you which plants survive your winters — not when to plant them.
  • Planting calendar for Starke, FL (Old Farmer's Almanac) — your own dates, by ZIP code: when to start seeds indoors, transplant out, and plant your last crop. For Starke in 2026 the Almanac puts the last spring frost around Mar 1 and the first fall frost around Dec 10 — check the link for the current year.

Watch

  • Epic Gardening — 9 Beginner Gardening Mistakes to Avoid — the nine mistakes that kill a first garden: wrong spot, bad light, no mulch, planting too close, planting at the wrong time.

Classes you can actually take

  • Florida Master Gardener Volunteer Program (UF/IFAS Extension) — 50+ hours of training through your county Extension office, then 75 volunteer hours in your first year. Fees vary by county — ask your local Extension office for the next class and the cost. This is the real credential for plant people in Florida.

Mycology

Learn to grow mushrooms — identification, sterile technique, and cultivation — safely and step by step. Mycology turns into gourmet and medicinal mushroom sales, kits, and cultures, all from home.

Free tools + know-how

  • FreshCap Mushrooms — Grow Mushrooms Easy in a 5-Gallon Bucket — the easiest first grow: a bucket, aspen wood chips, and oyster mushroom spawn. The video is on the page. Start here.
  • FreshCap Mushrooms — The 3 Levels of Mushroom Growing — level 1: start from a kit. Level 2: start from spawn. Level 3: start from culture. Move up one level at a time.
  • Mushroom Observer — free mushroom identification community — post photos and notes, and experienced members propose IDs and vote. Running since 2006. Slow, but it's the second opinion that holds up.

Classes + community

  • North American Mycological Association — Educational Programs — free identification workshops and video presentations, including how to use a key to ID mushrooms properly.
  • North American Mycological Association — Join — NAMA has 90+ affiliated mushroom clubs across the US and Canada. A local club is the safest way to learn foraging: go out with people who know.

Substrate + sterilization basics — don't guess this part

MaterialTreatmentTemp / time
Grain spawn (rye, oats, millet)Full sterilize in a pressure cooker121°C / 250°F at 15 PSI — 90 min for quart jars, 2.5–3 hrs for big bags
Supplemented sawdust blocksFull sterilizeSame 121°C / 15 PSI — 2.5–3 hrs
Straw / coco coir (oysters)Pasteurize — hot-water soak, no pressure cooker needed60–65°C (140–149°F) for 4–6 hrs, or a 65–85°C soak overnight (FreshCap bucket method)
Oyster bucket growLayer spawn + pasteurized aspen chips, keep cool + darkFully colonized in 10–21 days; fruiting needs humidity around 80–100%

Sterilization kills everything — that's what grain and rich substrates need, because any survivor outruns your mycelium. Pasteurization only knocks the competition back, which is enough for straw and coir with aggressive species like oysters. Times depend on load size — check the guides linked above for your exact setup. A regular Instant Pot-style cooker only reaches about 10–12 PSI (~240°F), not the 15 PSI / 250°F target — a stovetop pressure canner is the right tool for grain.

Safety, plain and simple: never eat a wild mushroom based on an app, a photo, or a guess. Get a local expert to confirm it in person, every time. This tab is about growing gourmet mushrooms at home — oysters, shiitake, lion's mane — not eating what you found in the woods.

What it leads to: gourmet mushroom sales, grow kits, cultures, and spawn — all from home, with a small footprint. Oysters are the beginner species: fast, forgiving, and they outcompete most contamination. Start with a kit or a bucket, sell your first harvest, then scale.

While Talk is on, your voice ducks the music to a quiet bed — the music follows your voice, and fades back up slowly when you stop. Tap Talk again to stop.

🎧 Live radio preview

When this is ON and headphones are plugged in, you hear the live mix (music + mic + guest + soundboard) in your headphones. On a phone the browser plays to the default audio device — headphones when plugged in, or Bluetooth. To get this mix on the air from your phone, run an aux cable from the phone's headphone jack into the PC's line-in and set BUTT to that input.

🥁 Soundboard

Eight pads — tap to fire a drop or jingle. Tap + file on a pad to load your own clip onto it instead.

Not connected. Plug in your AKAI MPK Mini II by USB (or Bluetooth), tap Connect, and its 8 pads + knobs link up automatically.

To go live from your phone: open the YouTube app (or your streaming app) → Go Live → paste the stream link here. This box just sets the link the video box plays — the phone browser can't push video directly into the site's video box. The video goes through YouTube Live / Twitch.

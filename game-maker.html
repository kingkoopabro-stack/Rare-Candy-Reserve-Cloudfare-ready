          💾 Save & share

          Your map autosaves on this device as you work.

            The play link opens the game on the play page. Very big maps may be too long for a link — then the game code is copied instead, and players paste it into the code box on the play page. Games save as .rcg project files; older .rpg.json files still open.

            🎵 Soundtrack

            Make your game’s music in the Studio DAW, then attach it here — it plays on loop while anyone plays your game.

            🎹 Open the Studio DAW (VN-StudioV2) ↗

            How to: 1) Open the DAW (it is also in the Music tab of the Learn hub). 2) Make your tune. 3) Click Export WAV in the DAW. 4) Pick that WAV file below.

            No soundtrack yet.

            WAVs up to 1 MB are saved inside your .rcg file and play for everyone. A bigger file is remembered by name only — keep tunes short and light so they travel with the game.

            🕹 How to play

            Move: arrow keys / WASD, or the on-screen d-pad.
            Talk / read signs: Space or E next to someone, or tap TALK.
            Chests & items: walk onto them.
            Battles: walk into an enemy — Attack, use a potion, or Run.
            Win: reach the portal, or collect the coins your goal asks for.

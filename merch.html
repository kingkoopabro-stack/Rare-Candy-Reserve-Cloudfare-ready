Fabric & quality

Soft, heavyweight, made to be lived in.

These are printed on soft, heavyweight, garment-dyed cotton — the good blanks, not the thin scratchy kind. Made to be lived in, not thrown away after a season. Unisex cuts, comfort fits, sizes S–3XL on tees and hoodies.

New baroque and whimsical designing — ornate moths and moon phases, botanical mushroom plates, scarabs and runes, mana trees and stained-glass suns. Old-world engraving mixed with neon. Every design starts from something I actually love.

How it works

Print on demand — no stock, no waste.

Nothing is printed until you order it. That means no boxes of unsold shirts sitting around, and every piece is made fresh for the person who bought it.

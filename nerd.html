⭐ First review · 10/10

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time — Remake.

Review · Nintendo Switch 2

Source: GAMINGbible

The remake we deserved long ago

This one plays incredibly well. Everything that made the original a classic still holds up after being remade — and the finally-added dedicated B button makes the controls feel right in a way they never quite did before. I could not recommend this game enough. When it comes to remakes, this one was long overdue, and it delivers.

The remake launched on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5, 2026 — and within 24 hours it was already running on PC through emulation, hitting 60fps in early tests. That’s how much people wanted to play it. The real way to play it is on Switch 2, where it belongs, but the hype is real either way.

10/10 🍬🍬🍬🍬🍬

🕹️ Up and coming — built here

Photosyn — the game from this shop.

A space-mushroom meteor falls on Shibuya. The Shibuya Bloom spreads — a vine infection, more plant than fungi — and the Bloomed walk the streets. Marche, Aiya, and Bastille fight back with a sword, a handmade revolver, and plant-powered weapons. Turn-based combat with Persona-style flair, a real Tarot system, Resyn magic, and a moral at the center of it: divide friend from foe — peace or violence. Every choice has an effect. Save who you can while you can.

Where it’s headed

Built in Unreal Engine 5. M-rated, Parasite Eve-style horror with a Ghibli heart and a gruesome story. Shibuya first — open world, revisitable — with crafting, resin magic, and a city you restore by helping people.

Nintendo Switch 2 Steam PC iOS mobile Android mobile

Aiming for Switch 2, Steam / PC, and a scaled-down mobile version for iOS and Android. The cover above is concept art — the final game look is still in development, and the footage shown is early development, not final gameplay.

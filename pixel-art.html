💾 Save & export

“Send to Harmony Engine” hands your sprite to the Game Maker on this device — open Game Maker next and it will be waiting. Downloads are PNGs with a transparent background.

❓ How to use

  1. Pick a palette colour (or your own colour) and paint by clicking or dragging on the grid. It works with touch too.
  2. Use the fill bucket for big areas, the eraser to take pixels away, and the eyedropper to grab a colour from your drawing.
  3. Made a mistake? Undo goes back up to 20 steps; Redo brings a step back.
  4. Check the live preview — try 8× and the animation to see how it reads in a game.
  5. Save named designs on this device, download a PNG, or hit “Send to Harmony Engine” to use it in the Game Maker.

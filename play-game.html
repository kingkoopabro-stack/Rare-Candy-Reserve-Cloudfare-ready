How to play

Move: arrow keys / WASD, or the on-screen d-pad. Talk / read signs: Space or E next to someone, or tap TALK. Chests & items: walk onto them. Battles: walk into an enemy — Attack, use a potion, or Run. Win by reaching the portal, or collecting the coins your goal asks for. If the game has a soundtrack, it plays on loop — use the soundtrack button under the game to mute it.

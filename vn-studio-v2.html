Beat tracks: Kick/Snare/HiHat/Clap 16-step grid. Melodic tracks: open the Piano Roll to draw a melody — try SilverSaw, Ballerina, Piano, Harpsichord, Electric Piano, Strings Pad, Bass Synth, Sytrus FM, 3x Osc, Sub Bass or fully-Custom VST. Voice tracks: record, upload an MP3, apply Auto-Tune with Retune Speed, Key/Scale, LFO, Humanize, Flex-Tune and Mix knobs, load VST-style effects from the VST Rack, slice your vocal into a rhythm, and chop it into a melody that plays in key with your beat. Export any single take as its own WAV, or the whole mix. Every channel has a filter, a 16-step filter-automation lane, and an A/B pattern you assign per bar in 🧩 Arrangement. Save your whole song as a project file and reload it later in this same DAW.
